How is the loss rate calculated?
The original calculator uses a fixed ~550 kcal/day deficit (≈1 lb / 0.5 kg per week). Here you choose the deficit. Weekly loss ≈ deficit × 7 ÷ 7,700 kcal per kg (≈3,500 kcal per lb). This is an estimate: real-world loss slows as you get lighter and varies with water, adherence, sleep and stress. Deficits above ~25% of TDEE make muscle loss and burnout more likely.
