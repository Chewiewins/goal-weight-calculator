Privacy: Chewie Calories Calculator

Chewie Calories Calculator is provided under the ChewieWins name. Contact: chewiewins@proton.me. Effective date: 15 November 2026.

Calculator entries and local storage

The calculator processes the age, sex, height, weight, estimated body-fat percentage, goals and nutrition values you enter on your device. The inspected calculator code does not send these entries to the developer. Theme and unit preferences, saved calculation history, a locally generated code, Advanced settings and daily tracker entries may be stored in the browser's local storage on your device.

You can use the app's history-clearing and daily-reset controls for those records. To remove all locally stored calculator-site data, use your browser's site-data controls for chewiewins.github.io. Removing the Android app may not remove data held by the browser. Deleted local entries cannot be recovered by the developer. Browser or device behavior may affect storage and backups; this policy does not promise that uninstalling removes every copy.

Hosting and browser services

The Android app opens the hosted calculator through a browser-based Trusted Web Activity. Loading online content sends normal connection information to GitHub, the hosting provider. GitHub says visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security, whether or not the visitor is signed in. The inspected calculator pages contain no advertising or analytics integration. This statement is about the calculator code, not a claim that hosting or browser services process no technical information.

Offline pages, external links and support

A service worker can cache calculator files for later use. Offline availability depends on successful caching and browser behavior. Following optional Ko-fi or X links, opening a wallet, or contacting support involves another service and its own privacy practices.

Messages you choose to send to support are received through email. Do not send unnecessary health information. Routine support messages are deleted 90 days after resolution, unless they need to be kept for legal or security reasons. To ask about or request deletion of a support message, contact chewiewins@proton.me.
