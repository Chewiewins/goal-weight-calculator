Privacy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Short version: we collect nothing. Goal Weight Calculator has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your data stays on your device

  • All calculations happen in your browser. Nothing you type (age, weight, height, goals and so on) is sent to any server.
  • Settings, theme choice and any tracker entries in the Advanced version are saved only in your browser's localStorage on this device.
  • Clearing your browser or site data, or uninstalling the app, deletes them. They are not backed up anywhere, and we cannot recover them.

Offline use

When you install the app, a service worker saves a copy of the app's own pages in your browser so it works without a connection. It stores only the app files, not your personal data.

External links

The Ko-fi link, the crypto donation addresses and the @ChewieWins link on X are external. If you follow them, those services' own privacy policies apply. Donation is entirely optional.

Health note

This tool gives general estimates, not medical advice. Talk to a health professional before making big changes to your diet.
