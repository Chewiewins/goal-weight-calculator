Goal Weight & Calorie Calculator — User Guide

Work out your goal weight from body fat, a daily calorie budget, and a safe pace. About 2 minutes to learn.

1 · Goal weight (Step 1)

  1. Pick your unit: kg, lbs or st/lb (stones + pounds, 1 st = 14 lb).
  2. Enter current weight, your estimated body fat % (use the image guide link in the app) and your goal body fat %.
  3. Results appear as you type (or tap Calculate): goal weight, fat to lose and a time estimate.

Lean mass = weight × (1 − BF%)
Goal weight = lean mass ÷ (1 − goal BF%)

Example: 80 kg at 25% → 15% gives 70.6 kg (lose 9.4 kg).

Decimals can use a dot or a comma. Changing unit does not convert numbers already typed — re-enter them.

2 · Calorie budget (Step 2)

  1. Choose sex, height (cm, or ft/in for lbs and st/lb), age.
  2. Pick an activity level: 1.1 = under ~9k steps, 0–1 workouts a week · 1.2 = 10–13k steps, 2–3 workouts · 1.3 = 14k+ steps, 3+ workouts · 1.4 = 18k+ steps, 3+ workouts.

BMR = Mifflin-St Jeor
Maintenance (TDEE) = BMR × activity

Your daily calorie target = maintenance − your chosen deficit.

3 · Safety warnings

  • Minimum intake: 1200 kcal/day (women), 1500 kcal/day (men). Below that you'll see a warning.
  • Deficit over 25% of maintenance raises risk of muscle loss, tiredness and rebound.
  • Time to goal also flags impossible inputs (date in the past, goal not below current weight) and suggests an earliest safe date.
  • Slower is usually easier to sustain. Estimates only — not medical advice.

4 · Choose your deficit — three ways

kcal/day
Type or slide a fixed deficit (e.g. 250 mild, 550 default, 750, 1000 aggressive).
% of TDEE
Deficit as a share of maintenance (10–25% is typical). Needs height and age.
Time to goal
Enter weeks or a target date (the two stay in sync; earliest date is tomorrow). The app works out the deficit needed:
kcal/day = fat to lose × 7700 ÷ days
(3500 per lb in lbs and st/lb) Example: 9.4 kg in 19 weeks ≈ 545 kcal/day.

Switching modes carries your deficit over (e.g. 550 kcal/day shows as ≈ 18.8 weeks). The Weekly loss rate and Time to goal cards update in every mode.

5 · Protein target (Step 3)

Tap Calculate Protein Intake for 1.6–2.0 g per kg of lean body mass — helps keep muscle while losing fat. Needs weight and current body fat %.

80 kg at 25% → 60 kg lean → 96–120 g a day.

6 · Using the file

  • Phone: chat apps and Drive previews often block scripts, so buttons do nothing. Download the file, then Open in Chrome or Safari (iPhone: Save to Files → open with Safari, or "Open in browser").
  • Desktop: double-click index.html; it opens in your default browser.
  • Keep the guide with the app, or use the User guide link in the app — it works even from a lone file.

Top-right switch: Light / Dark / Auto (Auto follows your phone or computer). Your choice is remembered.

Everything runs on your device — nothing is uploaded and it works offline.

7 · Add to home screen

  • Best: put the file on any free web host (e.g. GitHub Pages, Netlify Drop), open that web address, then:
  • iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen / Install app.
  • Without hosting, bookmark the file in your browser, or keep it in Files / Downloads.

Disclaimer: estimates assume consistent diet, training and TDEE. They ignore illness, plateaus, injury, stress and medical conditions. Speak to a health professional before large changes.
